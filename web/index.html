Checking

What is checked

  • Every file is re-hashed and compared with the signed manifest. A changed, missing or added file is reported by name.
  • The manifest signature, the forensic log hash chain and the chain of custody are recomputed, not read from the package.
  • The qualified eIDAS timestamp is verified offline: TSA signature, certificate chain and revocation at the time of stamping, and the TSA's status on the EU Trusted List at that moment.
  • The OpenTimestamps proof is replayed to its Bitcoin block and compared with block headers from independent sources.
  • Works for packages from the ProofSnap extension, the ProofSnap API and dashboard, matter exports and delivery archives.
  • What a valid result means: the files have not changed since they were sealed, and they existed no later than the time shown. It does not say who created the content or whether it is true.

Prefer the command line? npx proofsnap-verify package.zip
